How budget estimates work

Illustrative daily vehicle rates: £450 / £620 / £750 / £860. Departure month sets the multiplier: May–September ×1.25; April/October ×1.10; otherwise ×1.00. Guide allowance £220/day; driver overnight allowance £150/night/vehicle. These are planning assumptions, not confirmed supplier tariffs.

Optional road mileage is supplied by you, not calculated or verified here. Its allowance is £1.85/mile/vehicle. Crossings, tolls, parking, additional drivers, guide expenses and VAT treatment require quotation.
